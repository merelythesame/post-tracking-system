# Privacy Policy — Post Tracking System

## 1. Data Controller

merelythesame | GitHub: github.com/merelythesame

## 2. Data We Collect

- **Account data**: email address, name, password (hashed)
- **Shipment data**: sender/recipient info, addresses, tracking history
- **Session data**: session cookies for authentication
- **Support tickets**: messages and status history

## 3. Purpose of Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

Data is processed to provide shipment tracking services,
user authentication, and customer support.

## 4. Data Storage

All data is stored in a MySQL database managed locally
via Docker. No data is shared with third parties.

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR Art. 15–22)

- Right to access your data
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to data portability

To exercise your rights, contact the administrator
through the Support section of the application.

## 6. Cookies

We use session cookies for authentication purposes only.
Cookie consent is managed via the cookie banner.

## 7. Retention Period

Data is retained while your account is active.
Deleted accounts are purged within 30 days.
